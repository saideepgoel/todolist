# JEE Student To-Do List

## Daily Essentials
- [ ] Wake up at a fixed time and start with 15-20 minutes of light revision (formulae/reactions).
- [ ] Solve **Physics, Chemistry, and Math** every day (at least one focused session each).
- [ ] Complete 2-3 timed problem sets (mixed difficulty).
- [ ] Revise class notes the same day after coaching/school.
- [ ] Maintain an error log: record every mistake and why it happened.
- [ ] Practice 20-30 minutes of mental math/calculation speed.
- [ ] End the day with quick recap: what was learned, what is pending.

## Weekly Targets
- [ ] Finish one chapter-wise objective sheet per subject.
- [ ] Take at least 1 full-length mock test (JEE Main/Advanced pattern).
- [ ] Analyze every test deeply: classify errors into concept, calculation, reading, and time-management.
- [ ] Re-attempt incorrect and skipped questions without looking at solutions first.
- [ ] Update chapter progress tracker (Strong / Moderate / Weak).
- [ ] Revise weak topics on the weekend with focused practice.

## Subject-Wise Priorities

### Physics
- [ ] Build concept clarity from NCERT + coaching modules.
- [ ] Practice numericals daily from Mechanics, Electrodynamics, and Modern Physics.
- [ ] Memorize standard results, dimensions, and units.
- [ ] Redo difficult previous-year questions (PYQs).

### Chemistry
- [ ] **Physical Chemistry:** practice numericals and formula-based questions daily.
- [ ] **Organic Chemistry:** revise mechanisms, named reactions, and exceptions.
- [ ] **Inorganic Chemistry:** revise NCERT lines, periodic trends, and coordination compounds.
- [ ] Make short notes for reactions, reagents, and color/compound facts.

### Mathematics
- [ ] Practice problem-solving from Algebra, Calculus, and Coordinate Geometry every day.
- [ ] Keep a formula notebook and revise it every 2-3 days.
- [ ] Focus on accuracy in lengthy topics (Calculus, Probability, 3D).
- [ ] Solve PYQs by topic and difficulty level.

## Monthly Milestones
- [ ] Complete at least one full revision cycle of previously covered chapters.
- [ ] Attempt 3-4 full syllabus mock tests.
- [ ] Compare score trend and identify score-limiting chapters.
- [ ] Create an action plan for the next month (chapter targets + test targets).

## Exam Strategy Checklist
- [ ] Practice with strict exam timing and OMR/computer-based format.
- [ ] Develop attempt order (easy -> moderate -> difficult).
- [ ] Set section-wise time caps and follow them in mocks.
- [ ] Train yourself to skip ego questions quickly.
- [ ] Keep last 10-15 minutes for review and marked questions.

## Health & Discipline
- [ ] Sleep 6.5-8 hours consistently.
- [ ] Exercise/stretch for 20-30 minutes daily.
- [ ] Use focused study blocks (e.g., 50/10 or 90/15 method).
- [ ] Keep phone/social media blocked during study slots.
- [ ] Take one light break session weekly to prevent burnout.

## Last 60 Days Before Exam
- [ ] Prioritize revision + PYQs + mock analysis over new resources.
- [ ] Revise short notes daily (formulae, reactions, tricks).
- [ ] Solve previous 5-10 years' papers in exam conditions.
- [ ] Focus on high-weightage and high-accuracy chapters.
- [ ] Stabilize sleep schedule according to exam time slot.
